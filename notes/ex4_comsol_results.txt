EXERCISE 4 - COMSOL vs ANALYTICAL
====================================================================

COMSOL run (the course cluster, comsol/ex4_desktop/Ex4_FFF.java):
  3D channel 1000 x 100 x 100 um, 45 electrodes (23 x 10 V, 22 x GND)
  91 particles released on the y = 5..95 um grid at x = 0, z = 50 um
  121 output times, 0..120 s
  mid-channel particle (released at y = 50 um) is particle #45

Release check (t = 0):
  x = 0.0 .. 0.0 um   (expected 0)
  y = 5.0 .. 95.0 um   (expected 5 .. 95)
  z = 50.0 .. 50.0 um   (expected 50)

Analytical reference:
  Re{K}(10 MHz) = -0.083670  -> negative DEP
  y_eq = 31.980 um

Key comparison numbers:
  particle leaves the 1 mm channel at t = 18.0 s
     x = 955.4 um, z = nan um
  at t = 120 s (extrapolated beyond the channel):
     x = nan um, z = nan um
  max |z_COMSOL - z_analytical| while inside the channel = 1.198 um
  max |x_COMSOL - x_analytical| while inside the channel = 217.708 um
  mean |dz| = 0.805 um

Vertical motion (the physics that sets the equilibrium):
  max |z_COMSOL - z_analytical| inside the channel = 1.198 um
  mean |dz| = 0.805 um
  z when the particle leaves the channel = 40.25 um
  (equilibrium y_eq = 31.98 um, NOT reached)

Downstream motion:
  COMSOL dx/dt at release = 35.89 um/s
  analytical 2D profile   = 41.67 um/s   (ratio 0.861)
  square-duct centre peak = 37.78 um/s
  largest |dy/dt| at release = 5.94 um/s (wall particles drift towards the
  centreline; the centre particle's own drift is -0.01 um/s).  The
  COMSOL channel is 3D, the analytical reference assumes a 2D profile
  in z only.
  max |x_COMSOL - x_analytical| inside the channel = 217.7 um

Interpretation:
  The vertical physics - the part that actually sets the equilibrium -
  agrees to ~1.2 um over the whole in-channel travel.
  The downstream distance differs because the analytical reference uses a
  2D parallel-plate profile while COMSOL solves the 3D square duct, so the
  particle is carried faster and drifts in y as well.  Both agree on the
  headline point: the particle leaves the 1 mm channel long before it
  reaches y_eq = 32.0 um (COMSOL z at the outlet 40.3 um).
